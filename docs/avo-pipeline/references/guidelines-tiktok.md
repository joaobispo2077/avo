# Guidelines: --tiktok

TikTok and vertical short-form guidelines.

## Load

- [`AGENTS.md`](../../../AGENTS.md) retention without manipulation rules
- Hybrid: `youtube-format-playbooks` (short-form sections) + vertical safe margins

## Diagnosis checklist

1. **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 default; verify caption and overlay safe zones
2. **Hook window:** value in first 1–3 seconds without deceptive preview
3. **Density:** Level 2–4 motion; readable text when viewer must read
4. **Audio:** speech intelligibility on phone speakers; music ducked under dialogue
5. **Platform risks:** reused content, music rights, disclosure, misleading edits

## Vertical-specific

- Captions: high contrast, lower-third band, no face occlusion
- Punch-ins only when motivated; avoid empty cliffhangers
- Preserve pauses that carry comedy, sincerity, or emphasis

## Delivery notes

- Export progressive H.264; verify first frame is intentional
- Burned captions vs platform captions: pick one strategy per deliverable

## After guidelines

`/avo.trim` for tight vertical cut, `/avo.motion` for overlays, `/avo.pipeline` for full path.
