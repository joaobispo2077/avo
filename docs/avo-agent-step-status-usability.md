# AVO step-status usability protocol

## Status

Recruitment pending. No participant results are claimed yet.

## Purpose

Check whether a person can identify the current workflow position, the next
step, and the action or update that will move work forward without relying on
earlier chat history.

## Participants

Recruit at least five people who did not author the response contract. Include
at least two who are unfamiliar with AVO. Record experience level only after the
task so it cannot prime answers.

## Procedure

1. Randomize five de-identified AVO responses: start, progress, approval,
   blocked, and completion.
2. Show one response at a time without command documentation or earlier chat.
3. Ask, in this order: “What workflow is active?”, “Where is it now?”, “What
   happens next?”, and “What must you do, if anything?”
4. Do not explain terms, point to the footer, or correct answers during the
   session.
5. Record the participant's wording verbatim, then score it against the durable
   state used to create the response.

## Success rule

At least 80% of all question responses must identify the correct state and next
action without prompting, and every participant must correctly distinguish a
blocked prerequisite from a content failure. Any misleading response is revised
and re-tested with new participants.
